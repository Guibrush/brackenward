# T-0084 — The tower window is visible only at its final position

Owner: `eng/gameplay`
Milestone: G2 — Vertical slice
Wave: 2H — inventory interaction
Status: open

## Problem

Clicking a placed tower in the level makes the window appear at the top-right of the
screen for a frame or two, then snap to its anchored position. `open_for()` calls
`show()` before `_place_panel()` runs on the next process tick, so the first visible
frames are the scene's default rect.

## Fix

1. `src/ui/tower_window.gd`:
   - `open_for()` shows the window **at alpha 0** for one layout tick (a transparent
     Control still lays out, so wrapping labels measure at the real width), then
     `_place_panel()` sets the size and position and restores alpha 1. The player never
     sees the scene's default rect.
   - A hidden window cannot measure its wrapping labels (each word becomes its own line,
     measured at ~4600 px in practice), so show-transparent-then-place is the mechanism;
     hiding it is not.
   - `is_revealed()` is false until the placement, and pointer confirms before it are
     ignored. `close()`/`unbind()` cancel the countdown and restore alpha 1.
   - No other behaviour changes: anchor, outside-close, rows, Escape.
2. `src/ui/tests/inventory_migration_demo.gd`:
   - Open the window through a real click as today, and additionally prove the reveal
     order without relying on a screenshot: call `window.open_for(id)` directly on a
     closed window, assert `modulate.a == 0` and `not is_revealed()` in that same frame,
     then wait two frames and assert `is_revealed()`, open, panel rect inside the
     viewport.
   - Assert the invariant the bug violated: whenever the window is open, the panel rect
     has a positive size and is fully inside the viewport.
   - Keep every existing case green.
