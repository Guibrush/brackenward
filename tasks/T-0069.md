# T-0069 — The floating tower window

Owner: `eng/gameplay`
Milestone: G2 — Vertical slice
Wave: 2G — UI structure
Status: open

## Goal

One window for everything about a tower: what it is, its tier, what is equipped, and the
actions — equip an upgrade, upgrade its tier, take it back. Opened from the inventory bar or
from a placed tower.

## What the human decided

- Opens by clicking a tower in the **inventory bar** or a **placed tower** in the level.
- Shows the tower, its tier, and its equipped upgrades.
- **Equipping works on towers in hand as well as placed** — possession is enough.
- **Tier upgrades live here too**, bought with gold.
- **Read-only during a wave**: the window opens and can be read, but equip, tier-up and take-back
  are refused with the arena's own reason.
- The bar shows only **unequipped** upgrades, so equipping must remove the item from the bar and
  re-showing it must return it. Removal of a spent upgrade stays switched off per the earlier
  decision — an upgrade that is replaced is destroyed, and the window must say so before it
  happens if a slot is taken.

## Definition of done — one demo, real events, one frame

1. `--check-only`, pasted.
2. A demo on the **real boot path** with pushed events: clicking a tower in the bar opens the
   window showing its tier; equipping an upgrade from the window moves the item out of the bar's
   available list and onto the tower; equipping to a tower **still in hand** works; upgrading the
   tier spends gold and the window shows the new tier.
3. **Read-only during a wave:** with a wave running, the window opens and every action is refused
   with a printed reason, and gold and tier are unchanged.
4. **Planted failure:** with two slots filled, equipping a third must either be refused or replace
   with an explicit warning — demonstrate whichever the inventory implements, and show the demo
   catching a silent loss if the replacement path is used.
5. **A rendered frame** of the window open over a level, saved outside the repo, path reported.
6. `tests.ps1` 5/5 and short `smoke.ps1` unchanged.
