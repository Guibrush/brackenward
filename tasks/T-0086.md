# T-0086 — The tower window ends at its last action

Owner: `eng/gameplay`
Milestone: G2 — Vertical slice
Wave: 2H — inventory interaction
Status: open

## Goal

In the tower window, everything below the `Take back to the inventory` row is
unnecessary. Remove the status line (gold / phase), the refusal sentence label and the
hint line. The window ends with its three action rows.

## What changes

1. `scenes/tower_window.tscn`: delete the `Status`, `Reason` and `Hint` nodes under
   `Panel/Margin/Column`. The column is exactly `Title`, `Identity`, `Tier`,
   `Equipped`, `Rows`.
2. `src/ui/tower_window.gd`:
   - remove the `status_path`, `reason_path` and `hint_path` exports and the three
     `_set_label()` calls in `_apply_copy()`; delete the now-unused `gold_format`,
     `field_separator` and `hint_text` copy exports if nothing else reads them;
   - keep `_reason`, `_refuse()` and `reason()`: refusals still have a sentence for the
     log and for a caller, they are simply no longer drawn;
   - no behaviour change to equip, tier, take-back, read-only, anchor or outside-close.
3. `src/ui/tests/inventory_migration_demo.gd`:
   - `_check_window_paths()` keeps only the surviving paths (title, identity, tier,
     equipped, rows, panel);
   - add the same shape of assertion as the build panel: the window's column has
     exactly five children, `["Title", "Identity", "Tier", "Equipped", "Rows"]`, and
     there is no node at the old `Status`/`Reason`/`Hint` names.
   - Keep every existing case green.
