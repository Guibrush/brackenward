# T-0062 — Give the panel a viewport to anchor against

Owner: `eng/gameplay`
Milestone: G2 — Vertical slice
Wave: 2E — playability
Status: open

## The cause, established

`BuildPanel` is a `Control` whose parent is the level's `Arena` (`Node2D`). A Control resolves
full-rect anchors against its parent's rect; a `Node2D` has none, so the panel is **0×0**. The
column is then centred with negative y: `title y=-194`, `state y=-163`, `wave y=-132`, rows at
`-101/-65/-29`. Only the last child (Hint) falls inside the viewport — which is exactly the
frame the human saw.

Binding is fine: `bind_arena()` runs, returns true, three rows build (Start Wave, arrow,
bramble), labels fill. **This is layout, not binding.**

## The fix to implement

**Put the panel under a `CanvasLayer` in `scenes/main.tscn`.** A Control directly under a
CanvasLayer anchors to the viewport, so the panel gets a real rect in both windowed and
headless runs, and no viewport-size arithmetic is needed.

- Add `[node name="UI" type="CanvasLayer" parent="."]` and reparent `BuildPanel` under it.
- Update the panel's `arena_path` in `scenes/main.tscn` to reach the arena from its new depth
  (`^"../.."`), and fix any demo that resolves the panel by path.
- Delete the `_fit_to_viewport()` attempt from T-0061's incomplete pass — sizing from the
  runtime viewport is wrong headless (64×64) and unnecessary once anchors work.
