# T-0083 — Anchor the tower window at its icon and let a click dismiss it

Owner: `eng/gameplay`
Milestone: G2 — Vertical slice
Wave: 2H — inventory interaction
Status: open

## Goal

The tower window stops being a centred modal sheet. It becomes a compact popover
anchored at the click that opened it, with no full-screen dark backdrop, and a click
outside it closes it.

## What changes

1. **Scene** — `scenes/tower_window.tscn`:
   - delete the full-screen `Backdrop` `ColorRect` (the dimming layer);
   - delete the `Center` `CenterContainer`; `Panel` becomes a direct child of the root;
   - root `mouse_filter = IGNORE`; `Panel` `mouse_filter = STOP`, `layout_mode = 0`,
     `custom_minimum_size = Vector2(320, 0)` (was 560);
   - **every exported path in `tower_window.gd` moves from `Center/Panel/...` to
     `Panel/...`**. This is the L-041 trap: the demo must assert each path resolves.
2. **Script** — `src/ui/tower_window.gd`:
   - `open_for()` records the anchor point as `get_viewport().get_mouse_position()`
     (the click that opened it: a bar slot or a tower on the board) and the process
     frame it opened on;
   - after `show()`/`refresh()`, place the panel with a deferred/awaited layout: the
     panel's top-left is `anchor + (18, -panel.size.y - 18)` (above and right of the
     click), clamped so the whole panel stays inside the viewport with an 8 px margin;
     if the clamped result would cover the anchor, place it below instead;
   - expose `anchor_point() -> Vector2` and keep `panel_rect()` for the demo;
   - `_input()`: a pointer CONFIRM outside `panel_rect()` closes the window
     (`why = "clicked outside"`), consumes the event, and does nothing else. Ignore a
     pointer confirm that arrives on the opening frame, so the click that opened the
     window cannot immediately close it. Keyboard/pad confirm and dismiss behave as
     today; Escape still closes.
   - document the pad caveat: the anchor is the last mouse position, which is stale for
     a pad open; controller anchoring belongs to the controller task.
3. **Demo** — `src/ui/tests/inventory_migration_demo.gd`: the existing window cases
   gain:
   - every exported window path resolves to its node class;
   - the open panel is at most 360 px wide, smaller than the viewport in both axes, and
     fully inside the viewport;
   - its centre is within 320 px of the click that opened it (the measured panel is
     ~442 px tall at 320 wide, so the centre is ~300 px away; the point is that it is
     nearer the click than the viewport centre, not a fixed radius);
   - `Backdrop` is absent;
   - a click on an **empty board point outside** the panel closes the window and is
     consumed: free build slots, the arena's refusal count, the panel's selection and
     the bar's selection are all unchanged;
   - reopening and pressing Escape still closes it.
   Keep every existing case green.
