## Prompts (verbatim from art/director)

**Rime**

> a rime tower: a solid ice-and-frost siege tower, not a building. A sturdy square shaft
> of pale grey stone masonry (stone #8b8291, dusk #4a3b52, slate #5b8a8c) 28 to 32 px
> wide, occupying the full tile width. Low stone footing block at the base only: one flat
> rectangular block of plain grey stone directly under the shaft, 28 to 32 px wide,
> resting flat across the bottom row with a solid flat base, no diamond plinth, no
> tapering point, nothing beside it. The crown is a wide, bristling cluster of sharp
> faceted ice crystal shards branching outward to span 36 to 44 px wide across the top
> of the tower. Pale white-blue glints (highlight #fff4d6) appear sparingly on the
> sharpest crystal facet edges only, not on the stone shaft. The whole tower sits inside
> the canvas with clear 2 to 3 px margin at the top so no crystal tip touches or clips
> the top canvas border. A continuous 1 px #1b1a26 dark ink outline around the entire
> outer silhouette. No detached floating shards, no grass, no ground tile, no plinth, no
> platform, no shadow ellipse. Isometric 3/4 view, cold light from the top-left, isolated
> on a transparent background. Same rendering and line weight as art/style/anchor.png.

**Beacon**

> a beacon tower: a stocky stone signal brazier tower, not a tall house. A sturdy, wide
> square shaft of rough grey stone masonry at least 28 to 32 px wide, occupying the full
> tile width, shorter and stouter than a watchtower, with one small arched iron grate
> door low on the front face. The crown is a wide, flared circular iron brazier bowl
> (spanning 34 to 38 px wide, the widest part of the tower) mounted directly on top of
> the stone shaft. Inside the bowl burns a blazing fire: the flame is ONE SINGLE SOLID
> CONNECTED MASS of bright warm fire with smooth outer contours, no interior holes, no
> gaps between licks, and no detached floating sparks or embers. Low stone footing block
> at the base only: one flat block of grey stone directly under the shaft, resting flat
> on the bottom row, no plinth, no taper. The entire sprite is 52 to 56 px tall inside
> the 64x64 canvas, leaving a clear margin of 4 to 6 px above the tallest flame tip so
> nothing is clipped. The outer silhouette, INCLUDING the perimeter of the flame and the
> brazier bowl rim, is drawn with a continuous 1 px #1b1a26 dark ink outline. No grass,
> no ground tile, no plinth, no platform, no moss, no foliage green, no shadow ellipse.
> Isometric 3/4 view, warm dusk palette, strong light from top-left, isolated on
> transparent background. Same rendering and line weight as art/style/anchor.png.

## Verification and post

Reject before post for: a diamond/tapering base or plinth, content on row 0 (top
clipping), detached fragments, enclosed holes, boundary ink below 90%, pad greens
`#3a4a3a`/`#6f8f4a`/`#9ab55c`. Accept if height ≥80%, widest ≥24 px, pad-free including
outline.

Post accepted candidates: crop stray pad pixels only, palette snap if needed, complete a
small outline shortfall, binary alpha. Save under `art/candidates/`, register
`tower-rime-candidate` and `tower-beacon-candidate` (replace the rejected rime candidate
file; add the beacon candidate; keep the rejected raws as evidence), update the ledger
with credits and generations, and pass
`powershell.exe -NoProfile -File scripts/gate/assets.ps1` (L-048: never `pwsh`).
