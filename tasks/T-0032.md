# T-0032 — Regenerate the tower without the flat-cut crown

Owner: `art/pixel`
Milestone: G2 — Vertical slice
Wave: 2A — art correction
Status: superseded - hand-authored crown rejected; design changed to a roofed tower in T-0033

## Why

`assets/art/towers/arrow/idle/south.png` was accepted by `art/director`, but the human
rejected it on sight: the top of the tower is a flat cut. Measured, they are right —
the first opaque row is a 12-pixel horizontal bar at `y=7` with no merlon peaks, and the
rows below widen steadily. The crown is a slice, not a crenellated top. It was not
cropped by translation; the source render already started flat at `y=3`.

**Revised 2026-11-06 after two generations failed to fix it (L-036).** Attempt 1 at
img2img strength 220 reproduced the flat crown exactly (rows 13, 18, 25, 26, 33, 36, 36,
36); attempt 2 at strength 105 broke identity. The human's ruling: **the crown is
authored by hand, not regenerated.** Budget is 0 generations unless the director rejects
the authored version, in which case one generation may be requested with explicit
human approval.

## Goal

One regenerated arrow tower that reads as the **same tower** — same materials, same
proportion, same palette family — with a proper crenellated parapet visible at the top.

**Budget: 2 PixelLab generations maximum.** Stop at one if the crown is right.

## Method

1. Pass the current `assets/art/towers/arrow/idle/south.png` as the style/shape
   reference, so identity is preserved rather than reinvented.
2. Prompt for the crown explicitly: a complete crenellated parapet with distinct merlons
   and gaps, seen at the same 3/4 angle, the top surface fully inside the frame, nothing
   clipped by the canvas edge.
3. Remove the generator's ground pad, snap to the 16 locked hexes, apply the 1px
   `#1b1a26` outline across the **whole** outer silhouette including recesses, and fill
   enclosed alpha holes (both are standing batch rules).
4. Translate so the base sits on the bottom row. Nothing may be clipped at any edge.
5. Replace the `tower-arrow-idle-south` manifest entry — never a second entry. Record
   the true spend (including the failed candidates and this regeneration), the pad-free
   content box, and any remaining defect honestly.
6. Delete or register every file you create: `git status --porcelain -- assets/art/towers/arrow`
   must show no untracked path when you finish.

## Definition of done — including the defect that caused this brief

1. **Top-row profile proves the crown.** Print the width of each of the first 8 opaque
   rows. A flat crown shows a constant width (the rejected sprite was 12, 12, 14, ...);
   a crenellated one varies as merlons and gaps alternate. Paste the numbers.
2. The sprite otherwise matches the accepted one: same overall silhouette, same
   materials, same light direction, distinguishable from the anchor at thumbnail size.
3. Fill rule met after pad removal: height ≥ 85% of 64, width ≥ 32 px. Report both.
4. Zero off-palette pixels, binary alpha, no enclosed transparent holes, full ink on the
   outer silhouette. Paste the measurements.
5. `powershell -NoProfile -File scripts/gate/assets.ps1` exits 0; paste the output and
   the scanned count.
6. No untracked files left under `assets/art/towers/arrow/`.

## Constraints

- Write `assets/art/towers/arrow/**` and that one manifest entry only.
- Do not touch gate scripts, `art/STYLE.md`, or the anchor.
- No git commands; `studio-lead` commits with `Agent: art/pixel`, `Task: T-0032`.
- Zero free generations beyond the two budgeted, and no ElevenLabs calls.

## Return

The standard envelope with the row-width profile, the fill numbers, the gate output,
the cleanup proof, and SPEND as an explicit count.
