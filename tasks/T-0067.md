# T-0067 — Mount the inventory bar

Owner: `eng/gameplay`
Milestone: G2 — Vertical slice
Wave: 2G — UI structure
Status: open

## Goal

The inventory bar exists as a component (`src/ui/inventory_bar.gd`, `scenes/inventory_bar.tscn`)
but nothing mounts it. Mount it, bound to the live run, so the inventory is visible during a
run — on the map screen and in a level.

**Do not change `build_panel.gd` in this task.** The panel still lists towers; the two views
will briefly coexist. That duplication is deliberate sequencing: the game must never be left
with no inventory view at all. Removing the panel's tower rows is the next task.

## Context

- `src/ui/inventory_bar.gd` — `bind(...)`, `handle_input`, `tower_selected`, `claim_point()`,
  `force_view_for_demo()`, `use_live_inventory()`, `_verify()` with `INVENTORY_BAR_STALE`
- `src/ui/boot.gd` — owns the screens and the run; the natural place to mount a persistent bar
- `scenes/boot.tscn` — add a `Hud` CanvasLayer above the screens
- `src/ui/choice_modal.gd` — the opening choice; the bar should be hidden while it is up

## Scope

1. `scenes/boot.tscn` gains a `Hud` CanvasLayer; `boot.gd` gains an `inventory_bar_scene`
   export and mounts the bar there on `_start_run()`.
2. The bar binds to the live inventory and run state, and refreshes when they change.
3. Visible on the map screen **and** in a level; hidden while the opening choice is up, shown
   after it is confirmed (player and autostart paths both).
4. Freed with the run in `_release_run()`.
5. Clicking a tower in the bar raises `tower_selected` — no panel wiring yet, just the signal
   and the bar's own selection mark.

## Definition of done — one demo, real events, one frame

1. `--check-only`, pasted.
2. A demo on the **real boot path** with **real pushed events**: after the opening choice the
   bar shows the picked tower and upgrade with tiers and gold; it is absent while the choice is
   up and present after; a real click on the tower marks it selected and emits; the same bar is
   present in the level with the same contents.
3. `--autostart` still reaches a level with the bar mounted (the gate path).
4. **Planted failure:** bind the bar to a stale inventory in a copy and show `INVENTORY_BAR_STALE`
   being caught rather than drawn.
5. **A rendered frame** of the bar on the map screen and in the level, saved outside the repo,
   paths reported. The human judges appearance.
6. `tests.ps1` 5/5 and short `smoke.ps1` unchanged.
