# T-0107 — Generate swarmling, bloom and bulwark

Owner: `art/pixel`
Milestone: G2 — Vertical slice
Wave: 2J — art integration
Status: open

## Goal

Three sets, 4 directions, using `art/director`'s exact archetypes: **swarmling**
(size=16, 32×32), **bloom** (size=24, 32×32), **bulwark** (size=38, 48×48). No animation.

## Route

`create_character` standard, 4 directions, transparent. The tool pads the canvas; pad/crop
only transparent space to the target canvas, preserving every content pixel and leaving
clear margins (no content on row 0 or any edge). Post: palette snap ≤12, 1px `#1b1a26`
outline, binary alpha, 0 pad greens, 1 component, 0 holes. Credits fund the calls; the
plan counter is not the gate.

## Prompts (verbatim)

1. **Swarmling** — "pixel art character sprite of a tiny skittering spidery bracken bug,
   small compact round chitin body, six thin bristling legs, two tiny glowing amber eye
   dots, low crawling stance, small footprint, isometric 3/4 view, light from top-left,
   warm dusk palette, 1px dark ink outline #1b1a26, isolated on transparent background,
   wide clear margin around sprite, no ground tile."
2. **Bloom** — "pixel art character sprite of a bulbous crawling fungal spore creature,
   fleshy pulsating mushroom cap with glowing golden-amber spore speckles, short
   root-like crawling tendril base, distinct flared bell-dome silhouette, isometric 3/4
   view, light from top-left, warm dusk palette, 1px dark ink outline #1b1a26, isolated
   on transparent background, no ground tile, clear margins."
3. **Bulwark** — "pixel art character sprite of a massive stone-armored iron golem
   sub-boss, monolithic hunched brute, heavy interlocking stone slab plating across
   chest and shoulders, thick pillar stone arms, battering-ram horned stone crest,
   isometric 3/4 view, light from top-left, warm dusk palette, 1px dark ink outline
   #1b1a26, isolated on transparent background, no ground tile, clear margins."

## Save and register

`art/candidates/enemies/<kind>/idle/{south,west,east,north}.png` at 32×32 (swarmling,
bloom) or 48×48 (bulwark). Register one entry per kind (`type: character`, directions 4,
frames list, placeholder true, candidate, prompt, generator, seed/job, spend, metrics,
bbox). Run `powershell.exe -NoProfile -File scripts/gate/assets.ps1`.

## Constraints

- Three calls, no regenerations; stop on refusal with the exact response.
- No other enemies, animation, towers, wiring, git.

## Definition of done

Balance/spend, three sets with per-direction metrics, manifest entries, gate, one
recommendation per enemy.
