## Goal

Two calls: a corrected **mortar** retry (the T-0094 prompt leaked the anchor's grass
pad) and the **rime** candidate. One call each, no regenerations.

## Mortar retry — corrected prompt

Per L-045: the anchor keeps its grass diamond, and a footing that fills the frame
reproduces it. Ask for:

- a squat stone mortar tower with a wide, upward-facing bore;
- **a narrow stone footing block at the base only**, no slab, nothing side-to-side;
- no grass, no ground tile, no plinth, transparent background;
- the same route as before: `create_image_pro_flash`, 64×64, anchor
  `upload:25763f10-f6ff-47c2-8f2e-d9d0a7a82df3`, locked 16 palette list,
  `max_colors: 12`.

**Before any post pass**, scan the output for the three pad greens `#3a4a3a`,
`#6f8f4a`, `#9ab55c`. If any is present, reject the raw (register it as evidence) and
do not post it; the sprite is standing on the pad.

## Rime

Tall, narrow frost tower with crystal spires, cold palette from the locked 16 only,
distinct at thumbnail. Same route and reference. Generate it even if the mortar retry
is rejected, provided the balance still funds a call.

## Both, when accepted

Post: pad crop (only stray pixels, never a base), palette snap if needed, 1px
`#1b1a26` outline, binary alpha, no holes, no clipping, roof fill rule. Save under
`art/candidates/`, register (`tower-mortar-candidate`, `tower-rime-candidate`,
placeholder true, status candidate, prompt, generator, generations_spent, metrics, raw
evidence), and pass `assets.ps1`.
