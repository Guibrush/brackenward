# T-0057 — In-level build panel

Owner: `eng/gameplay`
Milestone: G2 — Vertical slice
Wave: 2E — playability
Status: open

## Goal

The player can actually play a level: pick a tower from what they own, put it on a tile,
upgrade it, take it back — and see their gold and HQ while doing it.

Without this, launching the game gives a board that plays itself.

## Context

- `src/gameplay/arena.gd` — `place_tower(pos, instance_id)`, `upgrade_tower(tower)`,
  `take_tower_back()`, the build-phase lock, `ARENA_REFUSED` with reasons,
  `last_refusal()`
- `src/systems/inventory.gd` — towers owned, tiers, upgrade slots
- `src/systems/run_state.gd` — gold and HQ current/max
- `src/ui/map_input.gd` — the input abstraction, already bound to `game_*` actions
- `src/ui/run_map_screen.gd` — the pattern to follow: data-driven rows, a single input
  route, refusals shown with the session's own reason string

## Scope

A panel over the level, not a new scene:

1. **List the towers the player owns**, with tier and equipped upgrades, marking which are
   already placed. Selecting one arms placement.
2. **Arm and place:** with a tower armed, clicking a build tile places it; a refused
   placement shows the arena's reason.
3. **Select a placed tower** and offer *upgrade* (with its cost) and *take back*, both
   refused with the reason when the phase is a wave or gold is short.
4. **Always visible:** gold, HQ current/max, and whether it is the build or wave phase.
5. Input through the same abstraction as the map screen; mouse and keyboard today, pad
   reachable by construction.

## Definition of done — with planted failures

1. `--check-only` per script, pasted.
2. A headless demo driving the real arena through the panel's API: arm a tower, place it on
   a free tile, see gold unchanged (placement is free), upgrade it and see gold fall by the
   cost, take it back and see the tile free and the instance back in the inventory at its
   tier.
3. **Planted failure A:** place with nothing armed — refused with a printed reason.
4. **Planted failure B:** upgrade during a wave — refused with the arena's own reason, and
   the gold is unchanged.
5. **Planted failure C:** upgrade with insufficient gold — refused, gold unchanged.
6. `tests.ps1` 5/5; `smoke.ps1` short probe unchanged (`stuck 0`, no error lines).
