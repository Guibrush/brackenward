# T-0104 — Generate the herald and warden sprite sets

Owner: `art/pixel`
Milestone: G2 — Vertical slice
Wave: 2J — art integration
Status: open

## Goal

Two static sets, 4 directions each: **herald** (sub-boss, 48×48) and **warden** (boss,
96×96). One `create_character` call per enemy, no animation.

## Prompts

- **Herald** (48×48): fast, banner/standard-bearing sub-boss; the speed aura must read
  in the silhouette (streaming cloth/banner), clearly larger than the normals but
  smaller than the warden.
- **Warden** (96×96): the run's exam — imposing armoured boss, 3× a normal enemy,
  readable as the largest silhouette on the board; armour and mass, no extra abilities
  drawn.

4 directions, transparent, palette-consistent bracken dusk, top-left light, no
ground/plinth/shadow.

## Post

Palette snap ≤12 tones, 1px `#1b1a26` outline, binary alpha, 0 pad pixels, no clipping
or detached fragments. Save `art/candidates/enemies/<kind>/idle/{south,west,east,north}.png`,
register one entry per kind (`directions: 4`, frames list, placeholder, candidate,
prompt/generator/seed/spend/metrics), run `powershell.exe -NoProfile -File scripts/gate/assets.ps1`.

## Constraints

- Two calls, no regenerations; stop on refusal with the exact response.
- No other enemies/animation/towers/wiring/git.

## Definition of done

Paths, per-direction metrics, balance/spend, manifest entries, gate, one recommendation
per enemy.
