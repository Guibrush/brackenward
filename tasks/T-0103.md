# T-0103 — Generate the swarmling, bloom and bulwark sprite sets

Owner: `art/pixel`
Milestone: G2 — Vertical slice
Wave: 2J — art integration
Status: open

## Goal

Three static sets, 4 directions each: **swarmling** and **bloom** at 32×32, **bulwark**
(sub-boss) at 48×48. One `create_character` call per enemy, no animation.

## Inputs

`docs/design/systems/enemies.md`, `art/STYLE.md`, the T-0102 entries for prompt/route
shape, and the credits (the plan counter stays at 4; credits fund the calls).

## Prompts

- **Swarmling**: tiny, low, mass-readable creature; must read as one of a pack at 32 px
  and not be confused with the grunt.
- **Bloom**: healer silhouette — a plant/bloom-like body with a visible aura cue (a ring
  or petals), distinct from the swarmling; it heals others, not itself.
- **Bulwark** (48×48 sub-boss): bulky, heavily plated, slow; the armour must read in
  silhouette at 1× and dominate the normal enemies without reaching boss size.

4 directions, transparent, palette-consistent bracken dusk, top-left light, no
ground/plinth/shadow.

## Post

Palette snap ≤12 tones, 1px `#1b1a26` outline, binary alpha, 0 pad pixels, no clipping
or detached fragments. Save `art/candidates/enemies/<kind>/idle/{south,west,east,north}.png`,
register one entry per kind (`directions: 4`, frames list, placeholder, candidate,
prompt/generator/seed/spend/metrics), run `powershell.exe -NoProfile -File scripts/gate/assets.ps1`.

## Constraints

- Three calls, no regenerations; stop on refusal with the exact response.
- No other enemies/animation/towers/wiring/git.

## Definition of done

Paths, per-direction metrics, balance/spend, manifest entries, gate, one recommendation
per enemy.
