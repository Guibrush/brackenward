# T-0097 — Generate the beacon and bramble tower candidates

Owner: `art/pixel`
Milestone: G2 — Vertical slice
Wave: 2J — art integration
Status: open

## Goal

The last two tower calls: **beacon** and **bramble**, one `create_image_pro_flash` call
each, funded by the $10 credit (the vendor charged T-0096's call to credits while plan
generations remained at 4, so the credit is the valid path; do not pre-flight-stop on
the plan counter).

## Route

- 64×64, transparent, anchor `upload:25763f10-f6ff-47c2-8f2e-d9d0a7a82df3`, locked 16
  palette list, `max_colors: 12`, prompt under 2000 code points (L-046).
- **Beacon**: tall stone lighthouse/beacon tower with a metal brazier or lantern crown,
  warm top-left-lit fire against the dusk palette, distinct from the roofed arrow, the
  ballista frame, the squat mortar and the ice rime.
- **Bramble**: overgrown thorn tower — dark green foliage and thorns over a stone core,
  using only leaf/deep green/sunleaf plus the stone/ink tones, silhouette distinct at
  thumbnail.

## Reject before post

- Three pad greens `#3a4a3a`, `#6f8f4a`, `#9ab55c` (L-045) for a *ground* pad, not for
  legitimate foliage: for bramble, foliage is allowed; a **grass diamond/plinth** is not.
  Judge shape, not just colour: a base that tapers to a point or reads as a ground
  diamond is a reject even with 0 green pixels (the T-0096 rime base is the example).
- Silhouette boundary ink coverage below 90% (L-047); complete the outline in post only
  when the shortfall is small and the silhouette is intact.
- Content touching the top canvas edge (clipping); content on the bottom row is expected
  (feet on the tile).
- Fill rule: height ≥80%, widest row ≥24 px, pad-free including outline; no detached
  floating fragments.

## Post and register

Crop stray pad pixels only, palette snap if needed, complete 1px `#1b1a26` outline,
binary alpha, no enclosed holes. Save under `art/candidates/`, register
`tower-beacon-candidate` and `tower-bramble-candidate` (placeholder true, status
candidate, prompt, generator, seed/job, generations/credits spent, metrics, raw
evidence), and pass `assets.ps1`.

## Constraints

- **Two calls, no regenerations.** If a call is refused for funding or the tool fails,
  stop and report the exact response; the human will decide on more credit.
- No enemies, animation, scene wiring or git.
- Record the credit and plan-generation balances before and after.

## Definition of done

- Both candidates or the stop reason, with paths, prompts, metrics and gate.
- Balance/spend before and after.
- One recommendation per candidate.

## Return

The standard envelope with paths, metrics, spend and `FILES TOUCHED`.
