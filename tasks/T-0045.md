# T-0045 — Wire the run into the arena

Owner: `eng/gameplay`
Milestone: G2 — Vertical slice
Wave: 2C — integration
Status: open

## Goal

The arena stops being a standalone scene and becomes one level of a run: it asks
`RunState` for HQ and gold, asks `Inventory` what the player has, and reports back when a
level is won or lost.

## Context

Read first, all of it verified and passing:

  `win_run()`, `damage_hq()`, `add_gold()`/`spend_gold()`
- `src/systems/inventory.gd` — towers carry tier and upgrades; `place_tower()` /
  `take_tower_back()` are free
- `src/systems/tower_instance.gd`, `upgrade_instance.gd`
- `src/gameplay/arena.gd`, `tower.gd`, `hq.gd`, `gold_ledger.gd` — the existing scene
- `docs/adr/0006-run-level-count.md`

## Scope

1. The arena constructs or receives a `RunState` and an `Inventory`; HQ health is **seeded
   from** the run state and every leak routes back through `damage_hq()`.
2. Placement draws from the **inventory**, not from a purchase: placing costs nothing and
   a taken-back tower keeps its tier and upgrades.
3. Gold flows to and from `RunState` — kills and level completion in, tier upgrades and
   anything bought out. The existing `GoldLedger` may become a view over the run state's
   balance, or be retired; say which and why.
4. **HQ carried between levels**: finishing a level with 7 of 10 HP means the next level
   starts at 7.
5. **Defeat** ends the run through `RunState`; the arena must not invent its own ending.
6. Level completion calls `complete_level()`; it must not call `win_run()` — the map owns
   that decision.
