# T-0029 — Canvas-fill probe

Owner: `art/pixel`
Milestone: G2 — Vertical slice
Wave: 2A — unblocks batch art
Status: in-progress - candidates on disk, closure pending

## Goal

One tower sprite that fills its 64×64 canvas at the declared 32 px scale. The wave-1
probe occupied roughly 32×41 of 64×64 and read squat; at that fill, 30 sprites would
inherit a silhouette that fails the readability pillar.

**Budget: 1–2 PixelLab generations.** Report the actual count. A second attempt is
allowed only if the first misses canvas fill, and the reason must be stated.

## Work

1. Generate the basic arrow tower, isometric 3/4, on transparent background, filling
   the frame: the subject should occupy roughly 52×60 or more of the 64×64 canvas,
   comparable to `art/style/anchor.png`.
2. Snap to the 16 locked hexes with the free workbench, add the 1px `#1b1a26`
   silhouette outline, crop or erase any generator ground.
3. Register it in `art/manifest.json` as a placeholder with real prompt, generator,
   cost and a `raw` reference to the untouched output.
4. `art/director` reviews it against `art/STYLE.md`: silhouette readable at 1×,
   distinguishable from the anchor and the wave-1 probe at thumbnail size, consistent
   light direction.
5. **Adopt exactly one candidate.** The manifest entry carries licence, prompt,
   generator, cost in generations, and the pad-free content box. The other candidate
   is **deleted together with its `.import` file**.
6. **No untracked residue.** `git status --porcelain` shows no file under
   `assets/art/towers/arrow/` that this task created and did not register or delete.
   A task that leaves unregistered images on disk is not done, even if the gate passes
   over them (`AGENTS.md`, "Art tasks").
7. **Fill rule** (`art/STYLE.md`): pad-free height ≥ 85% of the canvas and width ≥
   32 px. Report both numbers for the adopted sprite.

## Constraints

- You may write `assets/art/towers/arrow/**` and your own manifest entry; do not
  touch other entries, the anchor, or `art/STYLE.md`.
- No git commands; `studio-lead` commits with `Agent: art/pixel`, `Task: T-0029`.
- Do not modify `scripts/gate/assets.ps1` or any gate.

## Definition of done — with a planted failure

1. `assets.ps1` exits 0, with the scanned-image count pasted.
2. The sprite's content bounding box is reported as numbers (width × height of
   opaque pixels), and compared against the anchor's.
3. **Planted failure:** set one pixel to an off-palette colour, show `assets.ps1`
   exiting 1 and naming the colour, then restore it byte-for-byte and show the pass
   plus a hash match.
4. `art/director` verdict stated plainly: accepted for batch, or rejected with the
   specific reason. A rejection keeps batch art blocked.

## Return

The standard envelope with the bbox numbers, both gate runs, the director verdict,
and SPEND as an explicit generation count.
