# T-0085 — A bar press opens the tower window only on release

Owner: `eng/gameplay`
Milestone: G2 — Vertical slice
Wave: 2H — inventory interaction
Status: open

## Problem

Pressing a tower in the bar opens its window immediately; when the press becomes a
drag, `tower_drag_started` closes it again. The player sees the window flash for a
frame or two before the drag starts. The press must wait to learn whether it is a click
or a drag.

## Contract

- Add `signal tower_clicked(instance_id: int)` to the inventory bar.
- A press on a tower slot (in hand **or placed**) records a click candidate and keeps
  today's selection/arming.
- On release:
  - if a drag is live: `_finish_drag()`; a release that is **not** a drop (inside the bar,
    or under `drag_threshold_px`) emits `tower_clicked` for the carried id;
  - if no drag is live but a candidate is recorded (a **placed** slot, which does not
    drag), emit `tower_clicked` for it;
  - a press anywhere else, a cancelled drag, `unbind()` or the wave lock clears the
    candidate without emitting.
- The boot opens the tower window on `bar.tower_clicked`, not on `bar.tower_selected`.
  `tower_selected` keeps its one job: `panel.select_instance` on press.
- The board door (`panel.tower_inspected`) is unchanged.
- `tower_drag_started` still closes a window that was open for another tower.

## Scope

1. `src/ui/inventory_bar.gd`: the signal, the candidate state, release handling.
2. `src/ui/boot.gd`: mount-time connection to `tower_clicked` (fall back to
   `tower_selected` only if the signal is absent, so an older bar still works); report
   the choice in `BOOT_WIRED` or the mount print.
3. `src/ui/tests/inventory_migration_demo.gd`:
   - a real **press** on an in-hand slot leaves the window closed; a **release without
     motion** opens it (the new case);
   - the T-0078 drag case asserts the window stays closed from press through drop;
   - the T-0080 T-0079 case: remove the "the drop's press opened the window" assertion
     and assert instead that the press did not open it;
   - the placed-slot case: the window opens on release, before the take-back click;
   - the wave-lock case: the press does not open it; the release does (read-only).
   Keep every other case green.
