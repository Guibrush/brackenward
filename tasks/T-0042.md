# T-0042 — Store stock and between-level offers

Owner: `eng/systems`
Milestone: G2 — Vertical slice
Wave: 2B — offers
Status: open

## Goal

Two sources of new towers and upgrades: the **store** node's stock and the **between-level
offer**. Both draw from the same tables, both can be rerolled for gold, and both can be
switched off independently.

## Rules, from the human and from `docs/design/systems/`

- **Store:** sells towers **and** upgrades. Stock randomised per visit, **max 5 items**
  (data), tier-weighted so higher tiers are rarer.
- **Hidden rule, second half:** a store visited in the **second half of the run** is
  guaranteed to contain at least one **tier-2-or-better** item.
- **Offers:** a between-level choice of items, same draw machinery.
- **Reroll:** applies to both the offer and the store stock, costs gold, **competes with
  purchases**, and is **toggleable independently at each site**.
- Nothing is placed or equipped here — this object only decides what is on offer and what
  a reroll produces. The inventory (T-0041) owns what the player holds.

## Constraints

- Write `src/systems/offers.gd` and `src/systems/store.gd` (plus one test file,

- Every count, weight and toggle is a named field with a `PLACEHOLDER` marker.
- **40 steps, last 8 reserved** for verification and the envelope.
- No git commands; `studio-lead` commits with `Agent: eng/systems`, `Task: T-0042`.

## Definition of done — with planted failures

1. `--check-only` per script, pasted.
2. Selftest passing, one summary line.
3. **The second-half guarantee is observed, not asserted:** across at least 200 seeded
   stores split into first half and second half of a run, every second-half store contains
   at least one tier-2+ item. Print the counts for both halves.
4. **Planted failure A:** with store reroll toggled off, a reroll is refused with a printed
   reason and the stock is unchanged. With the offer reroll toggled off and the store's on,
   the store still rerolls — the two switches are independent.
5. **Planted failure B:** a draw that would exceed the stock size is refused rather than
   silently truncated.
6. Reroll cost is deducted through a seam the caller supplies (do not reach into a wallet
   directly); show an insufficient-gold reroll being refused.

independence evidence, and `FILES TOUCHED`.
