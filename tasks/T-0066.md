# T-0066 — Bottom inventory bar

Owner: `eng/gameplay`
Milestone: G2 — Vertical slice
Wave: 2G — UI structure
Status: open

## Goal

The inventory is visible **all the time during a run**, anchored at the bottom: towers and
upgrades as separate groups with their tiers, paged with a page indicator, with gold shown
beside it. Present on the map screen and inside a level.

## What the human decided

- Bottom of the screen, always visible during a run — **map screen too**, not level only.
- Towers and upgrades as **separate groups**, each item showing its **tier**.
- **Equipped upgrades do not appear** in the upgrades group.
- **Paged**, 8 slots per page by default, the size exposed as a setting.
- **Gold sits near the inventory**, in the bottom area. HP, wave readout and Start Wave stay
  in the left panel.
- Clicking a tower selects it — the floating tower window is the **next** task, so this one
  only needs to raise the signal and mark the selection. Do not build the window here.
- During a wave the bar is **visible and readable**, but every modifying action is refused;
  this task has no modifying actions of its own beyond selection.

## Context

- `src/systems/inventory.gd` — towers with tier, upgrades in hand, equipped state
- `src/systems/run_state.gd` — gold
- `src/ui/build_panel.gd` — the existing level panel; it currently lists towers and owns the
  armed state. Decide what moves to the bar and say so: the bar becomes the inventory view,
  the left panel keeps HP, wave and Start Wave plus the armed status line.
- `src/ui/run_map_screen.gd` — the map screen the bar must also appear on
- `src/ui/boot.gd` — owns the screens, so it is where a shared bar is mounted

## Constraints

- Write `src/ui/**` and `scenes/**` only; no `src/systems/**`, `scripts/**`, `docs/**`,
  `project.godot`.
- **40 steps, last 8 reserved** for verification and the envelope.
- No git commands; `studio-lead` commits with `Agent: eng/gameplay`, `Task: T-0066`.
- Zero generations.

## Definition of done — one demo, real events, and a frame

1. `--check-only`, pasted.
2. A demo on the **real boot path** with **real pushed events**: after the opening choice, the
   bar shows the picked tower and upgrade with tiers, gold beside it; clicking the tower selects
   it; with more than 8 items the page controls move between pages and the indicator follows.
   Build the >8 case by granting items through the inventory in the demo.
3. Equipped upgrades are absent from the upgrades group — prove it by equipping one and seeing
   the bar's count drop.
4. The bar is present on the map screen and in the level, same data both times.
5. **Planted failure:** make the bar read a stale inventory and show the demo catching the
   mismatch rather than displaying wrong items.
6. **A rendered frame** of the bar at the bottom of the level, saved outside the repo, path
   reported.
7. `tests.ps1` 5/5 and short `smoke.ps1` unchanged.
