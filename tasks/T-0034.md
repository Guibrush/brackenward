# T-0034 — Less flat body on the roofed tower

Owner: `art/pixel`
Milestone: G2 — Vertical slice

Status: rejected - straight course lines across a 3/4 face read as flat rules, not masonry; shipped sprite kept unchanged

## Why

The roofed tower shipped in T-0033 reads flat in the body: the shaft is largely one
`#8b8291` stone tone with little masonry variation below the eaves, which the manifest
already discloses as identity drift from the previous sprite. The human asked for it to
be less flat before Wave 2B starts.

**Budget: 0 generations.** This is a post pass on the existing sprite; regeneration is a
separate decision if the post pass is not enough.

## Work

1. Take `assets/art/towers/arrow/idle/south.png` as the source.
2. Add masonry variation to the flat stone areas: 2–3 horizontal course lines and a
   handful of block seams, using only the locked palette. Keep the established top-left
   light — darker tones (`#6b4a5a`, `#4a3b52`, `#2e2a3b`) on the shaded right and lower
   faces, lighter (`#f6d9a0`, `#edb05f`) reserved for lit edges.
3. Do not change the silhouette: **zero pixels on the outer boundary may change**, and
   the roof, door, arrow slits and plinth stay as they are.
4. Keep it restrained. The goal is "readable masonry", not noise; a handful of deliberate
   seams beats per-pixel dithering.

## Where the files go — no shipping yet

Write the result only to `%TEMP%\opencode\t0034\south-body.png` plus a 6× preview
`south-body-6x.png`. **Do not write to `assets/` and do not touch `art/manifest.json`.**
The human sees the image and approves before anything is committed (L-038).

## Definition of done

1. Before/after counts: pixels changed, all inside the body region (none on the
   silhouette boundary); tones before and after, with the cap of 12 respected; off-palette
   must stay 0.
2. Layout unchanged: content box, base row, roof rows, door and slit coordinates
   identical before and after except where masonry was added.
3. Fill figures under the amended rule (roofed: height ≥ 80%, widest row ≥ 24 px,
   measured including the 1 px ink outline) — unchanged.
4. 6× preview written and its path reported.
5. `git status` unchanged by your work; nothing written outside `%TEMP%`.

## Return

The standard envelope with the before/after numbers, the diff region, the preview path,
and a plain statement of whether the body still reads as flat to you. The human decides.
