# T-0068 — Retire the panel's tower list

Owner: `eng/gameplay`
Milestone: G2 — Vertical slice
Wave: 2G — UI structure
Status: open

## Goal

One inventory view, not two. The bar owns the inventory now; the panel keeps HP, the wave
readout, Start Wave and the armed/selected status line, and stops listing towers and gold.

## Context

- `src/ui/build_panel.gd` — still builds `KIND_TOWER` rows, still prints gold in its state line
- `src/ui/inventory_bar.gd` — mounted, owns the inventory view, raises `tower_selected`
- `src/ui/boot.gd` — mounts the bar; the natural place to wire the two together
- A previous attempt at this migration **broke the panel** (`KIND_TOWER` and
  `placed_marker`/`unplaced_marker` referenced after deletion) and had to be reverted. Parse
  after each edit, not at the end.

## Scope

1. Remove the panel's tower rows, its gold from the state line, and the now-dead helpers that
   only tower rows used. Nothing may reference a deleted identifier — that is exactly what broke
   last time.
2. **Pointer guard**: a click on the bar must not also be treated as a board click by the panel.
   `inventory_bar.gd` already exposes `claim_point()`; give the panel an `add_pointer_guard()` and
   consult it before acting.
3. **Wire the two directions**: bar `tower_selected` → `panel.select_instance(id)`; panel
   `tower_selected` → bar `mark_selected(id)`.
4. Fix the overlap visible in the last frame: the bar clipped the panel's hint line. The panel's
   content should stop above the bar.

## Constraints

- Write `src/ui/**` and `scenes/**` only; no `src/systems/**`, `scripts/**`, `docs/**`,
  `project.godot`.
- **40 steps, last 8 reserved** for verification and the envelope.
- No git commands; `studio-lead` commits with `Agent: eng/gameplay`, `Task: T-0068`.
- Zero generations.

## Definition of done — one demo, real events, one frame

1. `--check-only` on every touched script, pasted, **after the last edit**.
2. A demo on the **real boot path** with pushed events: the panel shows no tower rows and no
   gold; the bar shows both; clicking a tower in the bar selects it in the panel; clicking a
   placed tower in the level selects it in the bar; a click on the bar does **not** reach the
   board.
3. **Planted failure:** remove the pointer guard in a copy and show the demo catching the
   double-handled click.
4. **A rendered frame** of the level showing panel and bar without overlap, saved outside the
   repo, path reported.
5. `tests.ps1` 5/5 and short `smoke.ps1` unchanged.

## Return

The standard envelope with the frame path, the two-way selection evidence, the double-click
demonstration, and `FILES TOUCHED`.
