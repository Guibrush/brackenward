## Goal

Towers behave like towers: the player can place one on a buildable tile, upgrade it
through three tiers, sell it, and the tower shoots the enemy its targeting rule selects.

## Context

Read first:

- `scenes/main.tscn`, `src/gameplay/arena.gd`, `src/gameplay/lane_walker.gd`,
  `src/gameplay/hq.gd` — the existing scene and its contracts

  perks are locked, only behaviour changes

- `docs/LESSONS.md` — L-033 (no GDScript deliverable without `--check-only`),
  L-035 (the scene exits 0 even when broken, so assert on output)

## Scope

- A buildable-tile check: towers go on marked tiles, never on the lane.
- Placement, upgrade through three tiers, sell at a stated refund.
- One targeting rule set: first, last, strongest; the tower picks a target accordingly.
- During a wave: **no placement, upgrade or sell** — the input is refused with a reason.
- Towers fire on a cooldown and deal damage through the existing enemy contract.

Use placeholder numbers (cost, damage, rate, range) with a single `PLACEHOLDER` marker
near each, so T-0035's tables can replace them without hunting.
