# T-0033 — Roofed tower probe

Owner: `art/pixel`
Milestone: G2 — Vertical slice
Wave: 2A — art correction
Status: done - candidate A shipped by human choice, fill rule amended for roofed towers

## Why

The arrow tower's open battlement cannot be made to read at this scale. The generator
draws the top as a flat quad with no far wall, so hand-authored merlons float above it
(L-038, and the human rejected the result twice). The human's ruling: **change the tower
to a roofed one**, which plays to what the generator does well — its earliest attempts at
this subject produced convincing pitched roofs.

This is also a pipeline exercise: the human wants the team practised at producing a
shippable sprite before batch production starts.

**Budget: 2 PixelLab generations.** Two candidates, distinct roof shapes, so the human can
choose. One is acceptable if it is clearly right.

## What to produce

A tall stone watchtower with a **roof**, not a battlement:

- Candidate A: steep **conical or pyramidal roof**.
- Candidate B: **gabled pitched roof**, ridge running front-to-back.

Both must keep the tower body the approved sprite already has: stone shaft, arrow slits,
arched door, top-left light, 3/4 view, 64×64 canvas.

Prompt for the roof explicitly: roof smaller than the wall footprint, a visible overhang,
eaves line, no flat roof slab, no crenellations or merlons, nothing clipped by the canvas
edge. A demonstrably good roof beats a novel shape.

## Post-processing, all candidates

- Remove the generator's ground pad; no shipped sprite carries its own ground.
- Snap to the 16 locked hexes; max 12 tones; binary alpha.
- 1px `#1b1a26` ink on the whole outer silhouette including recesses.
- Fill enclosed alpha holes.
- Translate so the base sits on the bottom row; nothing clipped at any edge.
- Fill rule: pad-free height ≥ 85% of 64, width ≥ 32 px.

## Where the files go — no repo writes yet

Write both candidates only to `%TEMP%\opencode\t0033\candA.png` and `candB.png`, plus
upscaled previews at 6×. **Do not write to `assets/art/` and do not touch
`art/manifest.json`.** The human sees the images first and chooses; registration happens
after that ruling (L-038).

## Definition of done

1. Two candidates exist in temp, each measured and reported as numbers: pad-free content
   box, fill percentages, tone count, off-palette count, alpha values, ground-pad pixels
   (must be 0), enclosed holes (must be 0), silhouette boundary fully ink (yes/no).
2. **Roof check:** print the width profile of the first 12 opaque rows for each candidate.
   A roof tapers inward toward the top; a flat cut holds or widens. This is the
   measurement that distinguishes the failure from the fix.
3. 6× previews written beside each candidate so the human can see them.
4. Nothing written outside `%TEMP%`, and `git status` unchanged by your work.
5. State plainly which candidate you would ship and why — but the choice is the human's.

## Return

The standard envelope with both candidates' numbers, the row profiles, the preview paths,
and SPEND as an explicit generation count.
